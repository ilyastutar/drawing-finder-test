Drawing Finder v0.5 — Step 1

Automatic high-resolution OCR zone tiling for Google Document OCR. No OCR is sent anywhere in this step.

Goal of this step: load one drawing, draw one or more reusable OCR zones, and let the browser automatically split each zone into Google-safe high-resolution tiles. Default output is 500 dpi, maximum 35 MP per tile, maximum dimension 9,500 px, with overlap so a tag on a tile boundary is not lost. Once this behaves correctly, Step 2 will send these exact tiles automatically to Google OCR.
